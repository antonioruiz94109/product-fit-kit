# Luggage research — 2026-11-07

This is a **curated 15-model US-market starter catalog**, not a claim to cover most of the market. It includes hard and soft construction, spinner and two-wheel mechanisms, and four manufacturers. Colors are not separate products. Underseat bags, sets, rolling duffels and prices are outside this selection. Shoes remain the default category.

## Fit-test limits and the probe decision

The fit test found published objective specifications, but did not establish market coverage, annual refresh frequency or universal measurement comparability. The owner authorized the full build with those limits. No airline acceptance or safety determination is offered.

The five-product probe used Travelpro Crew Classic Compact Spinner, Travelpro Maxlite 5 Spinner, Away The Carry-On, Briggs & Riley Essential 2-Wheel, and Samsonite Freeform. **Two of five size records were conflicting or unverified**, reaching the method’s demotion threshold. The size-fit benchmark was removed before expansion. Final filters are listed empty weight, construction and wheel mechanism; dimensions are display-only.

Independent review subsequently found a conflicting weight on Away The Carry-On. This makes weight unavailable for one of the original five, below the demotion threshold. Construction and wheel mechanism remain verified for all five. Editorial mappings group exact manufacturer wording into Hard/Soft and Spinner/Two-wheel; these group labels are not manufacturer performance claims.

## Exceptions and resolutions

- **Conflicting, hidden:** Away The Carry-On dimensions and weight; Away The Bigger Carry-On dimensions; Travelpro Maxlite Air V2 weight. The manufacturer statements disagree. We retained the source evidence in data for review and did not choose a preferred number.
- **Not yet verified:** overall dimensions for Samsonite Freeform, Outline Pro and UpLIFT Hardside. The pages print dimensions, but inclusion of wheels and handles was not established from the accessed manufacturer evidence. These are not “Not published” cases.
- **Not published:** none in this luggage catalog.
- **Compatible rounding:** UpLIFT’s precise description and rounded specification weight agree at the shown precision. Its note records that distinction.
- **Briggs & Riley:** US specifications were corroborated against manufacturer UK pages with matching SKUs for wheel/handle inclusion. Global Spinner’s matching Olive variant is available in the US data, although the US page defaults to another color. This is a model/available-variant comparison, not a selected-color price claim.

Manufacturer corroboration: [Essential 2-Wheel](https://briggs-riley.co.uk/products/essential-56cm-2-wheel-expandable-carry-on-navy), [Essential Spinner](https://briggs-riley.co.uk/products/essential-carry-on-spinner), [Global Spinner](https://briggs-riley.co.uk/products/global-carry-on-spinner). The [Samsonite carry-on guide](https://shop.samsonite.com/carry-on-guide.html) did not resolve the measurement-definition gap.

## Independent verification

A separate research context opened every product source and the manufacturer corroboration pages, checking labels, exact wording, measurements, identity and the editorial mappings. Its [field-by-field audit](verification.json) records confirmed, conflicting and not-found evidence. The two newly found Away conflicts were corrected before the final checks; the audit records those resolutions. This was an AI-assisted source check, not physical measurement or a certification.

The final catalog contains 53 Published records, four Conflicting records and three Not yet verified records. Source pages can change. Re-check every six months and keep unresolved evidence excluded. Choosing this manageable starter set does not prove that 15 models represent the whole US market or that model refreshes are annual.
